# Long Session Description
Last year at ASCEND and the AVIATION Forum 2024, we introduced the area of Generative AI (GenAI) to the conference in the session “Generative AI in Defense: is ChatGPT good enough for Aviation?”, notably LLMs, following ChatGPTs rippling impact to the market at the end of 2022. While the panel began setting out to answer whether GenAI was mature enough for the industry, the panel quickly highlighted the industry’s quick application of GenAI in the aviation and aerospace industries within the months which followed, applications of GenAI that could that engineers expected to come, and furthermore, their enthusiasm for these new applications of GenAI in the industry.

This year, we propose bringing a follow-on group of engineers and specialists working with GenAI in the industry to discuss where they believe GenAI can benefit the industry next. GenAI,  applications, particularly Large Language Models (LLMs), have only begun in the sector, with numerous applications left to be taken up.

This past year, the AIAA community shared applications of LLMs in aviation to summarize and regurgitate text information in the industry in several technical sessions: 

- AviationGPT demonstrated the ability to answer questions on runaway arrival/departure for airtraffic controllers systems via Digital Automatic Terminal Information Service (DATIS) at up to 95% accuracy versus the prior 39 – 50% of the status quo rule-based method. 
- Likewise, another group demonstrated the ability to provide succinct summaries of Aviation Safety Reporting System (ASRS) data, reducing critically time and increasing accuracy in interpreting human-in-the-loop (HITL) data.

These were the primary and most successful applications of GenAI, particularly LLMs, presented this past year. Herein we notice a particular trend: the primary technology involved are LLMs and text-to-text generation. Yet, GenAI has come to encompasses a broad range of working mediums that the industry is on the cusp of realizing. 

The discussion in this panel will focus on as many aspects of applying GenAI in aerospace as imaginable: from text, audio, video, and image generation, including each in tandem with each other.  A thorough brainstorm with the engineers at the forefront of GenAI in the industry will reveal where we should point GenAI next in aviation and aerospace.
